# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (not yet released)

The first version.

- Live ray tracer in the browser: reflections, glass, soft shadows, blurry reflections, soft focus, bloom.
- 46 ready-made worlds, 45 sky looks, 22 materials, 12 shapes with cutting and melting.
- Land: grown islands and ranges, hand sculpting, erosion, picture to land.
- 3D lettering: 7 typefaces, custom fonts, solid / outline / neon, rings and arches, words in the land.
- Dice with locks, share links, undo, saved views, timeline with keys.
- Design maker: covers, flyers, posters, postcards, wallpapers, banners, tickets, trading cards; 20
  layouts, 70 ready-made designs, CD / cassette / vinyl mock-ups.
- Music: song analysis with beat detection, live reaction, meter bars, filming a song with its sound.
- Loops, 360° pictures, `.glb` / `.obj` import and export.
- MCP server for agents.
- Installable, and opens offline once visited.
- Modes: 1994 mode, a world from any word, GitHub year island (with a profile banner Action), photo to world,
  screensaver, fly, look around (360°, phone tilt, VR), time-lapse.
- Embed anywhere (iframe or `<untitled-world>` tag), community gallery, render from the terminal.
- Pluggable shapes, with four new ones: capsule, gem, star, twisted column.
