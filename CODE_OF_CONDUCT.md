# Code of conduct

Be kind, be patient, and assume the other person means well.

- Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- People arrive with different amounts of experience. Answer the question that was asked.

If someone is making this project unpleasant for you, write to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/denonmaryland). Reports are kept private. The maintainer may remove
comments, close threads or block accounts to keep this a good place to make things.
